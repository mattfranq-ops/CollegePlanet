    About the data. This prototype runs on built-in sample data, not live API calls. Pay and growth figures are modeled on BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (May 2023 medians) and 2023–33 employment projections; skills, degrees and certifications follow O*NET-style requirements. Company lists, course codes, cert prices and campus clubs are illustrative examples for a typical U.S. public university.

    Planning rules: 120 credits to graduate, 15 credits a normal term, 18 max, and about 20% of remaining credits treated as free electives that can absorb gap courses.
